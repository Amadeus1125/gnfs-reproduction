# Data Access

The study uses the Wind Farm 1 wind turbine SCADA signals for 2017 from EDP Open Data. Select **Download data** on the [official dataset page](https://edp.com/en/innovation/wind-farm-1-wind-turbine-scada-signals-2017).

If the download link becomes unavailable, you can try contacting EDP's stakeholder relations team at [stakeholders@edp.pt](mailto:stakeholders@edp.pt) for guidance. [EDP contact directory](https://www.edp.com/sites/default/files/document/2026-11/EDP_9M26_Interim_Report.pdf#page=75)

We welcome inquiries about the data or about reproducing the study. Please contact the corresponding author, Ruiming Fang, at [fangrm@hqu.edu.cn](mailto:fangrm@hqu.edu.cn). We will do our best to help.
